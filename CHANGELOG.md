# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-21

### Added
- The Present: put up to five gifts inside and design it in the wrapping menu: paper colour and pattern (plain, stripes, dots, stars), ribbon colour, box shape (cube, tall, wide, small) and bow (bow, big bow, rosette, knot), with a live preview and a Surprise me button.
- Write who it is for: a name tag hangs on the front, and only that player can open it.
- Opening: you step up to the present and unwrap it with both hands. The bow comes undone, the lid flies off, the sides fold open and the gifts rise out, then drop as items. Other players see you reach in.
- Not your present? You get bumped back, the present wobbles and says whose it is.
- A present keeps its gifts, look and name when you break it, and whoever wrapped it can sneak + right-click to unwrap it again.
- Settings: only open presents from December 24 to 26, or let anyone open any present.
- Its own sounds: paper tearing open, the lid popping off with sleigh bells, a chime when the gifts come out, and wrapping (with subtitles).
- In 11 languages.
