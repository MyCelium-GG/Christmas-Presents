# Christmas Presents

**Christmas Presents** lets you wrap real presents for your friends. Put up to five gifts inside, design the wrapping yourself, write who it is for, and leave it under the tree. When they open it, they walk up to it and unwrap it with their own hands: the bow comes undone, the lid flies off and the gifts rise out of the box.

![Unwrapping a present in first person: both hands lift the red lid off the box](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/christmaspresents/img/unwrapping.png)

## Wrap a present

1. Craft a **Present**: paper all around, with string at the top (P S P, P _ P, P P P)
2. Place it and right-click it to open the wrapping menu
3. Put up to **five gifts** in the slots, then pick the look:
   - **Paper:** 16 colours, plain, striped, dotted or with stars
   - **Ribbon:** 16 colours
   - **Box:** cube, tall, wide or small
   - **Bow:** a classic bow, a big bow, a round rosette or a simple knot
   - Or press **Surprise me** for a random look
4. Write the name of the player it is for in **To:**, and press **Wrap it!**

![The wrapping menu: five gift slots, paper and ribbon colours, pattern, box shape, bow, a live preview and the To field](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/christmaspresents/img/wrapping-menu.png)

Everyone nearby sees the present change while you design it, and a little tag on the front shows the name.

## Open a present

Right-click a present with your name on it. You step up to it and open it with both hands, and the gifts pop out once the paper is gone. Your friends see you reach in too.

If a present is not for you, it does not open: you get bumped back, the present wobbles and tells you whose it is. No peeking.

![Five wrapped presents in different colours, shapes and bows, each with a name tag](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/christmaspresents/img/presents.png)

## Good to know

- A present keeps everything when you break it: its gifts, look and name. Pick it up, carry it to the tree, place it again
- Whoever wrapped a present can sneak + right-click it to unwrap it again and change something
- Settings: only open presents from December 24 to 26 (with a countdown until then), or let anyone open any present
- Works in singleplayer and on servers. Install it on the server and on every player's game
- Fabric, Forge and NeoForge, Minecraft 26.1 to 26.3
- Requires [Mycel](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mycel). Without it the game still starts, and Christmas Presents tells you in chat that Mycel is missing
- In 11 languages: English, German, Spanish, French, Dutch, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Italian, Japanese and Simplified Chinese
